Outreach

Bringing fluid mechanics to teachers, students, and hallway passersby.

BERET+D — research experiences for teachers

Berkeley's BERET+D program gives teachers — and teachers-to-be — the opportunity to work with cutting-edge research and help translate it into lesson plans and classroom materials. Prof. Mäkiharju is the PI of the BERET+D NSF project (award details), which was initiated by Prof. George C. Johnson and Dr. Elisa Stone.

To ask about current BERET+D opportunities, contact Arash Jamshidi: arijamshidi (at) berkeley.edu.

Hallway exhibits at Etcheverry Hall

Since moving to Etcheverry Hall 2115 in summer 2019, we have been building a display of experiments in the second-floor showcase windows. The plan is an interactive touchscreen that lets visitors and students not only learn about what they see, but cause actions — for example, launching waves of their choosing in a 16-ft wave tank visible in the lab's teaching area.
